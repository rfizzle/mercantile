Mercantile

A villager and trade overhaul for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric).

Pickup, names, reputation, trade cycling, sentry pylons, and more.

Download on Modrinth CurseForge GitHub

Features

A glimpse of what Mercantile changes. Full list →

Villager Pickup

Sneak-right-click a villager to pick it up. Full NBT — profession, trades, gossip — is preserved.

Villager Names

Biome-themed names are auto-assigned on spawn and always visible above the villager.

Trade Cycling

Refresh a villager's trade pool using emeralds and emerald blocks instead of breaking their workstation.

Reputation

A five-tier global standing with persistent gossip that travels with villagers across the world.

Sentry Pylon

An iron-fueled defense block that spawns temporary iron golems to guard your village.

…and many more.
