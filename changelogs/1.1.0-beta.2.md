This beta focuses on bell visualization polish and sentry pylon reliability, rounding out two of the mod's core systems before the stable release.

## Added

- Villagers within a bell's range now glow while you hold a bell, making it easy to see who falls under its influence.
- The bell-radius circle is now centered on placed bells rather than on the player, so you can survey coverage from any angle.
- Idle sentries now walk back to their pylon before despawning, giving a clear visual cue that they're standing down rather than vanishing in place.

## Fixed

- Bell radius circles and workstation-link particles no longer disappear at longer distances due to the game's 32-block particle limit.
- A sentry engaged with an off-screen threat no longer despawns mid-fight.
- Disabling a pylon with redstone now correctly pauses its scanning and lets its sentries time out naturally; previously the pylon kept running even when switched off.
- Breaking a pylon now properly dismisses its sentries instead of leaving them stranded with no home.
- JEI integration now loads correctly when JEI is installed.
